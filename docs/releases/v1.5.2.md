Local video player for Android: your own files, the phone's hardware decoders first with bundled FFmpeg decoders as the fallback, and phone-to-phone transfer over your own Wi-Fi. No account, no cloud.

## What's new

- **A Settings switch answers anywhere on its row** — every switch in Settings now toggles from its title and subtitle as well as from the control on the right, so a thumb no longer has to land on the small switch. TalkBack reads the row once, with the setting's name and its state, instead of announcing a second toggle beside it.
- **The change is measured on the emulator** — a test writes the control bar position through the app's settings, reads it back from storage, and taps the row in Settings → Playback to prove the tap reaches the stored value. The instrumented suite is 31 tests.
- **Unchanged** — 1.5.2 upgrades over 1.5.1 in place, signed with the same key. Nothing else in the app changed: the player, the library, the decoders and the transfer tab are the builds 1.5.1 shipped.

Full changelog: https://github.com/NarDecH/Android_VDO_Player/blob/main/docs/CHANGELOG.md

## Before you install

Minimum Android 8.0 (API 26). `INTERNET` is declared only so the Transfer tab can open local sockets — nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and a log leaves the device only when you tap Share or Save.
