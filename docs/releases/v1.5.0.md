Local video player for Android: your own files, the phone's hardware decoders first with bundled FFmpeg decoders as the fallback, and phone-to-phone transfer over your own Wi-Fi. No account, no cloud.

## What's new

- **The control bar can move to the top** — a hand, a desk stand, or the bottom of a phone held in landscape covers the seek bar and the buttons. The arrow button in the title bar moves them above the picture and puts the title bar on the bottom edge, so the close button is never behind the controls. The choice is remembered, and the no-sound cards, the stream panel and the player's own messages follow the bar to the edge it moved to instead of staying where they were.
- **Back goes back to the list** — pressing back while a video played closed the app, because nothing in the player handled the press: it fell through to the screen that hosts the whole app. It now closes what the player opened first — the stream panel, the speed, aspect or audio picker — and then returns to the list the video came from, with the video stopped.
- **Both are measured, not just written** — the emulator job now presses back in a host activity and watches it survive the press, and measures which half of the screen each part of the overlay is laid out in, so a later change to either has to keep them true. 29 instrumented tests on the emulator, 149 JVM tests.
- **Unchanged** — 1.5.0 upgrades over 1.4.0 in place, signed with the same key, and the download stays the size 1.4.0 made it.

Full changelog: https://github.com/NarDecH/Android_VDO_Player/blob/main/docs/CHANGELOG.md

## Before you install

Minimum Android 8.0 (API 26). `INTERNET` is declared only so the Transfer tab can open local sockets — nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and a log leaves the device only when you tap Share or Save.
