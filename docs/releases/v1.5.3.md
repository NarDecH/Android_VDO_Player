Local video player for Android: your own files, the phone's hardware decoders first with bundled FFmpeg decoders as the fallback, and phone-to-phone transfer over your own Wi-Fi. No account, no cloud.

## What's new

- **The player is usable with a screen reader** — every icon is announced by name, and the places that were hard to follow now say something a listener can act on. The seek bar reports the time a seek would land on ("0:30 of 1:30") instead of a bare percentage, and while a thumb is on the bar the time follows the target. The position and the length are read as one sentence rather than two loose numbers, the repeat button names the mode it is in ("Repeat: the whole queue"), the queue counter says "Video 1 of 3" instead of being read as "one slash three", and the speed, aspect ratio and audio pickers announce which row is already chosen instead of marking it with a tick alone.
- **A test keeps the labels honest** — the emulator suite fails if a control is added to the player without a spoken label, and pins the four wordings above. The instrumented suite is 35 tests.
- **Unchanged** — 1.5.3 upgrades over 1.5.2 in place, signed with the same key. Nothing else in the app changed: the library, the decoders, the transfer tab and the Settings rows are the builds 1.5.2 shipped.

Full changelog: https://github.com/NarDecH/Android_VDO_Player/blob/main/docs/CHANGELOG.md

## Before you install

Minimum Android 8.0 (API 26). `INTERNET` is declared only so the Transfer tab can open local sockets — nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and a log leaves the device only when you tap Share or Save.
